The AI Story in Four Eras

1 · The Dreamers

  1. 💭1950

    Turing: "Can machines think?"

    The imitation game, now called the Turing test.

  2. 🏷️1955–56

    "Artificial intelligence"

    The phrase appears in McCarthy's proposal for the 1956 Dartmouth workshop.

2 · Machines that follow rules

  1. 💬1966

    ELIZA

    The first chatbot. Follows word rules and understands nothing.

  2. ♟️1997

    Deep Blue

    Beats chess champion Kasparov by searching 200 million positions a second.

  3. ❄️1970s, late 1980s

    AI winters

    Big promises, not delivered, so interest and money dried up.

3 · Machines that learn from examples

  1. 🧬1958

    Perceptron

    Rosenblatt's learning machine, inspired by neurons.

  2. 🖼️2012

    AlexNet

    Learns to recognise photos, trained on 2 GPUs. Deep learning takes off.

  3. ⚫2016

    AlphaGo

    Beats Go champion Lee Sedol 4–1.

4 · Machines that talk

  1. 👀2017

    Transformer

    "Attention Is All You Need" (Google).

  2. 📚2018–2020

    GPT models

    GPT-3 (2020) has 175 billion parameters.

  3. 🗨️30 Nov 2022

    ChatGPT

    Anyone can chat with an LLM. Claude, Gemini and others follow.

GPT = Generative (creates text) · Pre-trained (learned by reading lots of text first) · Transformer (the attention design).

The big shift: from rules people write → to learning from examples, powered by lots of data and GPUs.

Remember: AI can be wrong or out of date. Check important facts. People stay in charge.
