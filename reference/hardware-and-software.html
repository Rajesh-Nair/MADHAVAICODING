Hardware, Software, CPU & GPU

Hardware: the parts you can touch (chips, screen, keyboard, wires).

Software: instructions that tell the hardware what to do (apps, games, your programs).

Input → Process → Output

CPU vs GPU

🧠 CPU💪 GPU
Like…a few super-clever chefsthousands of simple helpers
Best atstep-by-step jobs, any taskmany small jobs at the same time
Examplerunning apps, opening filescolouring millions of pixels, games, video, AI

Remember: both are made of billions of transistors, tiny on/off switches. On = 1, off = 0.
