# Python & AI for Madhav — Resources

## Knowledge

### Computer history (Phase 0)
- [Computer History Museum — Revolution: The First 2000 Years of Computing](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/)
  Museum online exhibit, from the abacus to the smartphone. Use for: any history fact in Chapter 1.
- [CHM — ENIAC](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/birth-of-the-computer/4/78)
  Use for: ENIAC facts (1943–45 build, about 18,000 tubes, 30 tons, 1,000× faster, retired 1955).
- [CHM — The Babbage Engines](https://www.computerhistory.org/babbage/engines/)
  Use for: the Difference Engine (1821 start; No. 2 designed 1847–49; built in 1991 and it worked).
- [CHM — Pascal Adder (Pascaline)](https://www.computerhistory.org/collections/catalog/102710252)
  Use for: Pascal's 1642 gear calculator.
- [CHM — Inventing the Transistor](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/digital-logic/12/273) · [Bell Labs — 1956 Nobel Prize](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/awards/1956-nobel-prize-physics/)
  Use for: the transistor (Dec 1947; Bardeen, Brattain, Shockley).
- [Intel — The 4004](https://timeline.intel.com/1971/the-first-programmable-microprocessor:-the-4004)
  Use for: the first commercial microprocessor (Nov 1971, about 2,300 transistors).
- [Apple — M4 chip announcement](https://www.apple.com/ca/newsroom/2024/05/apple-introduces-m4-chip/)
  Use for: modern transistor counts (about 28 billion).

### Inside a computer: hardware, software, CPU/GPU (Lesson 1.2)
- [Code.org & Khan Academy — How Computers Work](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/code-org/computers-and-the-internet)
  6 short videos made for middle school (CS Discoveries): binary, circuits, CPU, memory, input and output, hardware and software. Use for: Chapter 1 and the 2.1 binary/switch ideas. Main video: [CPU, Memory, Input & Output](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DKGZlaPlVLY).
- [NVIDIA — What's the difference between a CPU and a GPU?](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/2009/12/16/whats-the-difference-between-a-cpu-and-a-gpu/)
  The CPU has a few cores for serial tasks; the GPU has many cores for thousands of parallel threads. Use for: CPU/GPU facts; the 1.3 link to AI.
- [Mythbusters paint the Mona Lisa (re-upload)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwDPb3T8bOQ) · [backup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8_ZTvG1WQxM)
  Paintball robots at NVISION 08: 1,100 paintballs paint the Mona Lisa in about 80 ms. Use for: the parallelism hook. NVIDIA's official upload (`-P28LKWTzrI`) went private; checked 2026-11-01.
- [Microsoft — How to check your GPU](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/learning-center/how-to-check-gpu) · [DirectX blog — GPUs in Task Manager](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/directx/gpus-in-the-task-manager/)
  Use for: Task Manager steps. The GPU view needs a WDDM 2.0+ driver.
- [Britannica Kids — Computer (Kids level)](https://kids.britannica.com/kids/article/computer/352990): hardware and software for young readers.

### Video (kid-friendly-ish)
- [Crash Course Computer Science (PBS)](https://www.pbs.org/video/early-computing-crash-course-computer-science-1-n8djp3/), hosted by Carrie Anne Philbin
  40 short episodes, fast-paced (teen/college level). Watch together and pause. Use for: Chapter 1 and later hardware topics.

### Age-suitable further reading (for "🔭 Explore more" sections)
- [Britannica Kids](https://kids.britannica.com/): kid-level encyclopedia. "Kids" articles suit him to read alone; "Students" articles are a challenge read. Some articles may need a school or library login. Used: [abacus](https://kids.britannica.com/kids/article/abacus/351393), [computer](https://kids.britannica.com/students/article/computer/273764).
- [Science Trek — Computers](https://sciencetrek.org/topics/computers) (Idaho Public Television): STEM videos and facts made for elementary-age kids.
- [Smithsonian — Human Computer Project](https://womenshistory.si.edu/human-computer-project): the women who programmed ENIAC. Adult-level, read together.
- [Science Museum London — Babbage's Difference Engines](https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/objects-and-stories/charles-babbages-difference-engines-and-science-museum): read together.
