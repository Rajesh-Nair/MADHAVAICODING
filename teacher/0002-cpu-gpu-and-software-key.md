# Key — 0002 · Lesson 1.2 Brains and Muscles: CPU, GPU and Software

> Teacher only. Not linked from the lesson page.

## In-session answers

**Warm-up**
- Q1: A tiny switch that turns on and off
- Q2: ENIAC (1945, before the transistor in 1947)

**Paintball prediction:** the "GPU" robot finishes first. It fires 1,100 paintballs at once and paints the Mona Lisa in about 80 milliseconds.

**Human GPU race:** with 4 helpers, expect about 2.5–3.5× faster, not exactly 4×. Ask why it isn't a full 4×:
- people work at different speeds
- there's set-up time
- the team waits for the slowest person

This is a real effect in computing (and a Deep Space thought for later).

**Key question, "each square depends on the one before":** the team can't split the work, so one fast worker (a CPU) is better.

**Sandwich recipe:** a good answer has numbered single actions and no skipped steps (opening the jar, picking up the knife).
- Hardware: knife, bread, jar, plate, his hands.
- Input: bread and jam.
- Process: spreading and assembling.
- Output: the sandwich.

**Task Manager:** no fixed answers.
- *Doing nothing:* CPU and GPU low (single digits).
- *Video:* GPU's "Video Decode" or "3D" rises, and the CPU may rise a little.
- *Many tabs at once:* the CPU spikes.

Good observation: "the GPU wakes up for video, the CPU for opening lots of things".

**Exit ticket**
- Q1: Colouring millions of pixels all at once
- Q2: Instructions telling hardware what to do
- Q3: Input

## Homework: what good looks like

1. **Chip hunt:** 10 plausible items (TV, remote, washing machine, fridge, car key, watch, games console, router, toothbrush, microwave…). The 3 IPO tables must have the input and output the right way round. The most common error is listing the screen as input.
2. **Definition rewrite:** uses all 4 words correctly. For example: *"A computer is hardware that follows software instructions to take input, process it and give output."*
3. **Memory challenge:** abacus, Pascaline, Babbage's Difference Engine. This is spaced retrieval from 1.1.
4. **Explain it:** any correct analogy ("lots of helpers at the same time"). Record the family member's question; it's a good debrief detail.

**Scoring out of 10** (for the debrief):
- Chip hunt: 4
- Definition: 2
- Memory: 2
- Explain: 2
