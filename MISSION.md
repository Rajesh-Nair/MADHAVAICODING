# Mission: Python & AI for Madhav

## Why
Madhav is growing up in the age of AI. The goal is for him to **understand how AI works and build with it**, not just use it as a magic box. He'll do that by learning to program in Python and building, step by step, toward the ideas behind the AI tools he sees around him.

## Success looks like
- He can explain, in his own words, how computers went from counting machines to today's AI, and why GPUs matter.
- He writes his own Python programs (games, turtle drawings, data tools) without starter code.
- He uses AI assistants responsibly: he asks them to explain rather than to do his work, fact-checks them, and spots hallucinations and fake images.
- He loads a real space, science or animal dataset, explores it with pandas and matplotlib, and tells a story with charts.
- He trains, evaluates and compares ML models (linear and logistic regression, trees, ensembles, K-means, PCA) and can say which is better, and why.
- He builds a tiny neural network from scratch and explains how next-word prediction leads to LLMs like the ones he uses.
- He finishes and shows off four capstones: Space Explorer, Solar System Data Explorer, Animal Classifier, and a final project.

## Constraints
- Age 11. Background in Scratch only. Ahead of grade level in math (some algebra). Slow typist at the start.
- 2 sessions a week, about 45 minutes each, taught by a parent (ML practitioner). About 30 minutes of required homework per lesson.
- Windows PC. Thonny and Jupyter/VS Code.
- AI tools only with his parent present, on the parent's account.
- No deadline. Mastery over speed, but motivation is the thing to protect.

## Out of scope
- Web development, app stores, publishing anything online.
- Deep calculus or formal proofs. Math is taught as intuition plus just enough to code it.
- Him having his own accounts on AI services, or using AI to write his solutions.
- Hands-on image, video or music generation by him (concept and one demo by his parent only).
